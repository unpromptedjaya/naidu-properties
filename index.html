    Home / Property management

    Hand over the keys and hear from me once a month.

    I take the property the day it is empty and hand it back when you want it.

    The full cycle

    1

    Onboarding

    Photo and video record of every room, existing damage noted, repair estimate before anything is spent.

    2

    Pricing and listing

    Rent set against what nearby flats actually closed at. Listed with proper photographs.

    3

    Tenant screening

    ID, employment and previous landlord verified. You approve the name.

    4

    Agreement and move-in

    Agreement drafted and e-stamped, inventory signed, deposit collected before keys change hands.

    5

    Rent and repairs

    Rent chased and transferred. My own plumber, electrician and carpenter at fixed rates.

    6

    Inspections

    Two photo inspections a year and a monthly statement of money in and out.

    7

    Exit and re-letting

    Damages assessed against the move-in report, deposit settled fairly, next tenant lined up early.
